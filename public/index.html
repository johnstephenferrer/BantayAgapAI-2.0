Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guidance on barangay services, submitting a concern, and checking a request.

How do I track a request I submitted?
Enter the reference number you received after submitting your request or concern in the Track a Request section.
Where can I find the requirements for a barangay service?
Browse the Barangay Services section or ask the AI Barangay Guide about the service you need.
How can I submit a concern or complaint?
Use the Report a Concern form on this website. Keep the reference number shown after submitting so you can check it later.
Where can I find official barangay and government information?
Use the official government links beside these FAQs. For Barangay 135-specific details, follow the barangay's verified contact channel.
How do I request a Barangay Clearance?
Contact or visit your barangay hall to confirm the current requirements and fees, since they can vary by location and purpose. For Manila barangay information, start with the Manila Barangay Bureau.
Where can I find health services or emergency help?
For city health centers and public hospital information, visit Manila Health Services. For emergency contact numbers, check the official eGovPH Emergency Hotlines directory.

Resident Reporting Guide & Community Rules

Lost & Found

  1. Tell barangay staff what was lost or found, and when and where it happened.
  2. Add a clear description or photo. Keep serial numbers, ID numbers, and private details out of public posts.
  3. Turn found items over to barangay staff. Verify ownership with them before returning an item.
  4. Let staff know when the item is returned so the notice can be updated.

Missing Pets

  1. Share a recent photo, pet description, and the date and general area last seen.
  2. Use a safe contact method; do not publish your home address or other private details.
  3. If you spot the pet, share the location with the owner or barangay staff. Avoid chasing an unfamiliar or frightened animal.
  4. Update the barangay when the pet is safely found.

Missing Persons

  1. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact barangay staff or law enforcement directly; do not rely on a public post alone.
  2. Share an accurate recent photo and the time and general place last seen, with the family's permission.
  3. Keep phone numbers, home addresses, and sensitive personal details private. Send tips directly to the family or authorities.
  4. Notify barangay staff when the person is safely located so the announcement can be updated.

Community Posting Rules & Emergency Help

  • Post only accurate information; do not make accusations or share unverified claims.
  • Respect privacy and get permission before sharing identifiable photos or personal details.
  • Barangay staff may review notices and update them when an item or person is found.

For emergency contact information, check the eGov Philippines hotline directory.
